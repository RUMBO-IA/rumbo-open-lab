# Governance

## Roles

### Visitor
May read public materials, join Discussions, and open Issues.

### Contributor
May propose changes through a fork and pull request. This role grants no organization membership or repository write authority.

### Maintainer
A trusted person with repository-scoped write or maintain authority. Maintainers review contributions and preserve the Open Lab boundary.

### Owner
Organization/repository administrator responsible for access, security settings, licensing scope, and final governance decisions.

No role transition is automatic.

## Decision boundaries

`DISCUSSION != ISSUE != CONTRIBUTION != REVIEW != ACCEPTANCE != MERGE != DEPLOYMENT != PRODUCTION_AUTHORITY`

RUMBO Open Lab has no direct production deployment authority.

## Promotion out of Open Lab

Work that may be useful elsewhere in RUMBO must be separately reviewed and intentionally internalized into the destination repository. Open Lab checks do not substitute for the destination repository's canonical checks.

## Access

Public contributors should not need organization membership.

Repository write access is exceptional, scoped, and revocable.

Private Core and Closed Lab access are separately governed and are never implied by participation here.
