# Code of Conduct

RUMBO Open Lab is a public technical collaboration space. Participation requires respectful, professional conduct.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas, code, evidence, and trade-offs without attacking people.
- Be specific, constructive, and willing to revise a claim when evidence changes.
- Respect different levels of experience, backgrounds, languages, and working styles.
- Keep contributions relevant to the repository and its public collaboration scope.
- Protect privacy and security. Do not post credentials, customer data, private messages, doxxing material, or confidential third-party information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, sexualized harassment, deliberate disruption, impersonation, doxxing, spam, or publishing private/sensitive information without authorization are not acceptable.

Security research does not authorize testing unrelated RUMBO systems, private repositories, customer systems, or third-party services.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock threads, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its participants.

Moderation decisions do not grant or remove employment, partnership, production, repository, or organization authority beyond the specific GitHub action taken.

For sensitive security matters, use the private reporting path described in SECURITY.md rather than publishing exploit details in a public issue.
