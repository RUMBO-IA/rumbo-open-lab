# Developer Certificate of Origin

RUMBO Open Lab uses the **Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1 (DCO)** for inbound contributions.

Authoritative DCO text:
https://developercertificate.org/

A contributor signs off a commit by adding:

`Signed-off-by: Your Name <your-email@example.com>`

Use:

`git commit -s`

The sign-off certifies that you created the contribution, or otherwise have the right to submit it under the repository's open-source license, and that the contribution and sign-off are part of the public project record.

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for the repository's contribution rules, including AI-assisted and corporate contributions.
